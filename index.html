Who we are

About Import Auto Solutions 2

Import Auto Solutions 2 LLC is an auto transport and freight carrier based in Greensboro, North Carolina. We move cars door to door for dealers, auctions and private customers, and haul reefer, flatbed (including oversized loads) and dry van freight, mainly across the Southeast.

We keep it straightforward: clear quotes, honest timing, and a real person on the phone. Dispatch and transport operate around the clock, so your load keeps moving after the office closes.
